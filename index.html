    How Digital Logic Gates Work

    AND: output is 1 only when both inputs are 1. OR: output is 1 when at least one input is 1. NOT: output is the inverse of its input.

    In this crossing, Warning = Train Detected AND System Active: lights start only when a train is sensed and the system is on. NOT Train Detected shows the track is clear, which allows the barrier to rise after a short delay. Train Detected OR System Active shows whether any input is present at all.

    Sequence: detect → warning lights → barrier lowers → train passes → train clears → warning continues briefly → barrier rises → lights off. If the train reaches the stop line before the barrier is fully down, it waits.

    Educational simulation only. These simplified gates are not a certified railway safety system.
